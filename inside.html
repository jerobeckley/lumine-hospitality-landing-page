inside selva

a tour of the room.

selva is five surfaces — not five features. each one is designed to replace a pile of half-broken tools with something calmer, smaller, and more considered.

01 home 02 feed 03 communities 04 market 05 profile

01 / 05

home.

the room when you walk in.

a gentle welcome every time you open the app — who's online, which member is worth meeting this week, a hand-picked piece from the journal, the three events closest to you, and a single quiet prompt if you feel like writing.

  • curated daily highlights, chronological underneath
  • members to meet, chosen by pillar affinity and proximity
  • events for the week — rsvp in one tap
  • a weekly letter from the editors
  • no red badges, no push to return — calm by design

02 / 05

the feed.

a living magazine, not a scroll.

long-form essays, photo essays, audio, short films, interviews, playlists. editorially assigned across six pillars, released on a weekly cadence, readable without an account. the content is the product — and it has to stand on its own.

  • 6 pillars · 4–6 pieces per pillar per month
  • formats: essay, photo essay, interview, audio, film, playlist, guide
  • every piece has a named author and a real editor
  • save to library, annotate privately, share internally
  • no ads, no sponsored placements, no algorithm
read sample articles →

03 / 05

communities.

small rooms inside the room.

focused groups formed around a single interest — film photography in cdmx, long-distance running, ambient producers, deep-reading circles. proposed by members, approved by the team, kept intentionally small so conversation stays real.

  • member-proposed, editor-approved — not self-service
  • capped where intimacy matters (12 · 50 · 250)
  • threaded conversation, shared library, optional real-time chat
  • each community can host its own events
  • leave at any time — no social penalty

04 / 05

the market.

a short shelf of things worth owning.

a quiet marketplace of vetted independent businesses — small presses, studios, growers, makers. every business goes through a taste-and-values review before it can list. no mass-produced goods. a transparent percentage of every sale goes to philanthropy.

  • businesses vetted on alignment, not spend
  • limited drops, founder stories, studio origin notes
  • 15–25% commission, published on the page
  • a public philanthropy allocation for every transaction
  • no fast-fashion, no dropshipping, no mass print-on-demand

05 / 05

profile.

your identity, in full.

the profile page is a considered page, not a stats dashboard. pillar affinities, work you've made, communities you inhabit, essays you've published inside. no followers, no likes, no vanity metrics — because the incentive to perform is the thing we're here to leave behind.

  • name, place, pillars — the only identity signals
  • a short bio, editable, no character pressure
  • your contributions, in the order you made them
  • members you've met irl, privately marked
  • no follower count, no like count, no score

ready?

the door is open a few hours a week.

applications are read by a human, in order received. we reply to everyone — even when it isn't a match this round.
