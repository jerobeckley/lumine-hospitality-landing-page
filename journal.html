editorial principles

the rules we publish by.

  1. every piece has a human editor. nothing auto-published. nothing AI-generated.
  2. no sponsored content. a percentage of membership goes to pay writers — that is the model.
  3. slow cadence. four to six pieces per pillar per month. we'd rather under-publish than flood.
  4. full attribution. named writers, named editors, disclosed conflicts.
  5. corrections in public. when we're wrong, we say so, on the original page, above the fold.
