membership

how to join.

selva is invitation-only. applications are read by a human, in order received. the quality gate is at the door — not a price gate, not a scarcity gate, a taste gate. below is exactly what membership includes, what it costs, and how the flow works.

included in membership

what a member actually gets.

01

the journal, in full

every published piece across the six pillars. essays, photo stories, interviews, playlists, audio, film — annotated, saveable, readable offline.

02

the community

access to every room inside the app. member-proposed communities (film, running, reading, producing, cooking, more), each kept intentionally small.

03

events

weekly online talks, monthly real-world dinners in the first three cities (nyc, cdmx, lisbon), and quarterly member residencies.

04

the market

access to limited drops from vetted independent businesses — studios, presses, growers. a percentage of every sale is public and goes to philanthropy.

05

a physical card

numbered in sequence. cream, forest, and gold. arrives in the mail. not a status symbol — a quiet reminder you belong.

06

a real editor

every member can submit one piece per quarter for editorial review. accepted pieces go on the journal with the same byline treatment as any contributor.

pricing

one tier. everyone equal.

no free trial, no founders' discount, no vip. the price signals commitment and filters for the right people. it covers the editors, the writers, the infrastructure, and the philanthropy allocation at the end of every quarter.

monthly

$33/mo

billed every month. cancel any time inside settings. no pro-rating either way.

annual — preferred

$333/yr

one payment, three months free. members on annual are asked to vote on the philanthropy allocation each quarter.

a percentage of every dollar — membership and marketplace — goes to philanthropy. the allocation is published openly on our purpose and can only move upward.

how the invitation flow runs

from email to inside the room — in five steps.

  1. 01

    leave your email.

    a one-line form. no questionnaire yet, no marketing opt-in, no instagram field. the email is how we reach you when it's time.

  2. 02

    a member of the team replies.

    within two weeks during pre-launch, within 72 hours once we're open. we ask three short questions — nothing about income, nothing about followers.

  3. 03

    a human reads your answers.

    we're looking for taste, intention, and depth in any one field. you don't need to be famous or credentialed. you need to be paying attention to something.

  4. 04

    you get an invitation (or a kind note).

    we reply to everyone. if it isn't a match this round, we say so, and we tell you when to try again. we don't ghost people.

  5. 05

    you set up your profile and you're in.

    billing happens here. the welcome walkthrough is short — pillar affinities, a short bio, your card. ten minutes, then the room.

frequently asked

the short answers.

is there a free trial?

no. paying from day one is part of how the room stays what it is. we'd rather be a firm yes to fewer people than a soft yes to everyone.

is there a membership cap?

no numerical cap. the taste gate at the door is the real filter. we will not cap membership for the sake of scarcity theatre.

who reads my application?

a founding member of the team, by hand, in the order received. no forms are auto-scored. no LLM sees your answers.

what if i can't afford $33/mo?

we hold a small scholarship fund for creators and practitioners who can show the work. reach out via contact. no means-testing. we trust you.

can i bring a guest to events?

yes — most member events allow one guest. a few smaller ones are members-only. it's always on the rsvp card.

how does the cancel flow work?

one tap inside settings. no dark patterns. no phone calls. no "are you sure?" pop-ups eight deep. we keep your profile for 90 days in case you come back.

what about businesses?

businesses use a separate vetting process. they fill a short alignment document and the team reviews it. approved businesses get a business profile and can list in the market.

what about philanthropy?

a transparent percentage of every dollar — membership and marketplace — is published openly and paid out quarterly. the percentage can only move upward. members on annual vote on the allocation.
