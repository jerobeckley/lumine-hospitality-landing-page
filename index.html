A note  ·  Issue 001

Why this exists.

We got tired. Instagram became a supermarket. LinkedIn, a theatre. Every feed we opened asked for more than it gave back. So we started building something smaller — fewer people, better writing, and an actual off-switch.

Issue 001 is eight pieces across six subjects: art, sound, wellness, mind, entrepreneurship, nature. Each one commissioned, edited, and published by a person. No ads. No sponsored content. No notifications dragging you back.

If anything in here is worth your time, send it to someone. That's how this spreads.
