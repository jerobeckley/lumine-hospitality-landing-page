editor's letter  ·  issue 001

a room, not a feed.

We built selva because nothing else felt quite right. instagram became a supermarket. linkedin became a theatre. the feed format — once a way to stay connected — became a way to stay exhausted. so we started writing down the names of people we wished were all in one place, and the magazines we wished existed, and slowly a spec emerged.

this is the first issue. it is small on purpose. there are eight pieces in total, across six pillars, in five formats. every one is written by a named person and read by a named editor. none of them were commissioned to sell anything.

if any of it reaches you, send it to one person who pays attention. that's how rooms grow.

— jero beckley, founder
