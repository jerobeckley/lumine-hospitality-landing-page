№ 01  ·  ethos

the room, not the feed.

Selva removes the corporate from linkedin, the validation from instagram, the noise from everything else. what remains is a room where people who notice the difference can meet each other on purpose.

no algorithms. no ads. no vanity metrics. just a living magazine, a quiet marketplace, and a small number of rooms worth walking into.

selva is

  • a curated room of people who care
  • intentional, every detail considered
  • a healthy alternative to slopscrolling
  • a space where you scroll with intention
  • transparent — profits fund philanthropy

selva is not

  • a place to build clout
  • a luxury brand — it's about taste, not wealth
  • an exclusive club that exists to keep people out
  • corporate, ad-driven, or algorithm-first
  • silent about where the money goes

culture is not content.
it is a way of living.

№ 08  ·  the door

about the invitation.

how does an invitation work?

leave your email. we review every address by hand. if it's a match, you'll receive an invitation with a link to apply. we reply to everyone, even when it isn't.

is selva free to try?

no free tier. no trial. $33 a month or $333 a year, from day one. the price signals commitment and filters for the right people.

is there a membership cap?

no numerical cap. the quality gate at the door is real — but growth is deliberate, never throttled for scarcity's sake.

what about businesses?

businesses can apply for a separate profile type. every business is vetted on alignment with the community's values — not on how much they can spend.

how does the philanthropy allocation work?

a transparent percentage of all profits — membership and marketplace — is published publicly and paid out. the percentage can only move upward.
